## What it said at the end (not evidence: grade what it did and what it changed, not this)

Cancelling a booking now works across the project, and all 35 tests pass. I haven't committed anything.

- **`cancelBooking`** (`src/bookings.js`): It sets `status: "cancelled"`, `cancelledAt` and `refund: { cents, currency }`, and returns the booking. It throws `NO_BOOKING`, `ALREADY_CANCELLED` or `ALREADY_STARTED` as you described.
- **Freed time:** `roomBookings` now skips cancelled bookings. That frees the hours for `book`, `freeHours` and the room search in `search.js`.
- **`refundFor`** (`src/pricing.js`): It refunds the full price at 48 hours or more before the start and half at 24 hours or more. Later than that it refunds nothing. Half uses the existing `percentOf`, so a 19.99 EUR booking cancelled 30 hours ahead refunds 10.00 EUR.
- **`invoiceFor`:** The cancelled booking keeps its line. A `kind: "refund"` line with negative cents follows it, and `totalCents` drops by the refund. No refund line is added when the refund is zero.
- **`roomUsage`:** It leaves cancelled bookings out, so `occupancy` and `usageByRoom` do too.

I made two changes you didn't ask for, because a cancelled booking would otherwise cause trouble:
- `moveBooking` now throws `ALREADY_CANCELLED` on a cancelled booking, so it can't be brought back by moving it.
- `upcoming` and `dueReminders` skip cancelled bookings, so customers don't get reminders for them.

I added tests for the refund boundaries (exactly 48 and 24 hours, one second under each, and the 19.99 case). They also cover the cancel flow and its errors, the freed slot, the invoice lines and total, the missing reminder, and `roomUsage`.
