## What it said at the end (not evidence: grade what it did and what it changed, not this)

The quote for the fern cabin, Friday 2025-06-13 to Monday 2025-06-16, now shows 3 nights (Fri, Sat, Sun). The total is 470.00 EUR: 425.00 for the nights plus 45.00 cleaning. All 25 tests pass.

**Root cause:** `stayNights` in `src/stay.mjs` called `eachDay(checkIn, checkOut)`, and `eachDay` includes both end days. That counted the check-out day as a night. The file's own header comment says guests leave on the morning of the check-out day. I changed it to `eachDay(checkIn, addDays(checkOut, -1))` and added the `addDays` import.

**Other effects:** `src/availability.mjs` also calls `stayNights`. Before this fix, a booking probably blocked its check-out day, so a same-day turnover would have looked like a conflict. That should now be fixed too, but I didn't test it. The existing tests didn't catch the original bug, so I also didn't add a regression test. I can add one for `stayNights` and the quote if you want.
