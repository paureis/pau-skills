from cursor/plugins pstack (MIT, Lauren Tan), taken as close to word for word as this repository's rules allow. The
owner finds both effective and has nothing that does the same job:
- `reframe` ([upstream](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/41e400932eca14f289e7db717a70455256b6d388/pstack/skills/reframe/SKILL.md)) teaches the expert words for what the user is building: 8 to 12
  terms ranked by how much each changes what gets built, each with a real project, paper or system and a link, two or
  three neighbouring fields that solved the same shape of problem, what the user did not mention, and their
  description rewritten with the new words. Every link is opened and confirmed; a citation that cannot be confirmed is
  cut. New skill, planning plugin.
- `sharpen` ([upstream](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/41e400932eca14f289e7db717a70455256b6d388/pstack/skills/sharpen/SKILL.md)) makes a task deeper, not bigger: what a top professional would do
  that the user did not ask for, the rawest material, the methods and tools, and what outside check would show the
  result is right; then it rewrites the prompt, asking the agent to prototype what an experiment can settle and leave
  product and preference choices to the user. It **replaces** our `skills/planning/sharpen` (adapted from an older
  pstack sharpen at 09d06eb; restate, confirm in six lines, then work), which was never started in the owner's last
  30 days.
